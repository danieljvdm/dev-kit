# Apple-inspired interfaces

Translate the relevant interaction principles to the target platform and input
devices. Preserve the product's design language and web accessibility.

- Dragging, sheets, springs, or interrupted motion:
  [interaction](references/interaction.md).
- Translucency, depth, spacing, or typography:
  [appearance](references/appearance.md).

Judge the result in the running interface. Make the relationship between input
and response clear, keep content legible, and preserve continuity across states.
Use native platform conventions where they fit; desktop and touch interactions
need not have identical controls or feedback.
